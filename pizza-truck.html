Pizza Truck Panic

Serve pizzas at each stop, then drive to the next one without letting the kitchen fall apart. Solo mode: no helpers, just pedals and a hydraulic clamp.
Keys while driving: A/D or arrows steer, W/S gas and brake, Space clamp. In the kitchen use the mouse.
